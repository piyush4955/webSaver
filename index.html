HOW TO INSTALL IN 60 SECONDS

No build tools or command line required. Pure vanilla JavaScript loaded directly into Chrome.

01

Download & Unzip

Click the Download Extension button to get web-content-saver.zip, then extract it to a folder on your computer.

↓ Download .ZIP
02

Open Chrome Extensions

In Google Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions and toggle on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

03

Click "Load unpacked"

Click the Load unpacked button on the top-left and select the extracted bootcamp4 folder.

04

Pin & Save Anywhere

Pin the extension to your toolbar. On any website, select text and press Alt+Shift+S to save notes with personal context!
